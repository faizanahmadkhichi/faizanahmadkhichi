# FAK LAB — Product Brief

[Open the live platform](https://faizankhichi.me/) · [Browse the complete directory](https://faizankhichi.me/tools)

## Overview

FAK LAB is a browser-based utility platform covering developer workflows, security, OSINT, digital forensics, networking, images, documents, media, text and encoding. The public directory currently brings together more than 90 focused tools behind one consistent interface.

## Product goals

- Make useful technical workflows accessible without installing desktop software.
- Keep navigation understandable even as the tool catalogue grows.
- Support mobile screens and limited-bandwidth conditions.
- Give every tool a focused purpose, direct route and clear entry point.

## Selected capabilities

- Image, audio and video forensics workflows
- PDF conversion, generation, metadata and organization tools
- Developer utilities for JSON, JWT, hashes, regular expressions and encoding
- Network and OSINT utilities including DNS, WHOIS and metadata inspection
- Everyday tools including translation, dictionaries and accessibility-friendly text utilities

## Engineering considerations

The product has to balance a large catalogue with discoverability, responsive layouts, browser capability differences and privacy-aware processing. Category hubs, searchable navigation and direct tool URLs keep the platform useful to both first-time visitors and returning users.

Product direction, interface design, full-stack development, deployment and continuous improvement.
